# GearVerdict changelog

- New: upgrade notice when a better item lands in your bags (re-checked on level-up, closes when the item leaves your bags)
- New: item quality option, two-step talent build picker, armor value and vendor arrows
- Talent builds for all classes, based on Wowhead guides
- Weapons, heavier armor and cloak armor are now scored; spec lookup fix
- Control Panel: refreshed look and larger window
